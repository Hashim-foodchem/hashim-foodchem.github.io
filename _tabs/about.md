I am a food scientist with a PhD in food engineering. My research focuses on dairy and plant-based proteins, fermentation, encapsulation of bioactive compounds, and the rheological and techno-functional properties of food systems.

## An international path

My training and research span several countries, which has shaped how I approach science: with different perspectives, methods and research cultures.

- **Egypt:** MSc in food science (dairy technology), Kafrelsheikh University.
- **Russia:** PhD in food engineering, ITMO University, on encapsulated date seed bioactive compounds in non-fat yogurt.
- **Finland:** postdoctoral research in food science at the University of Helsinki, on protein functionality, fermentation and plant-based foods.
- **International collaboration:** research collaboration with South Dakota State University (USA) and Istanbul Technical University (Türkiye).

## Research interests

- Dairy technology and dairy alternatives
- Ice-structuring proteins and cereal-based systems
- Plant-based and fermented proteins (yeast, faba bean, pea, soy)
- Rheology and techno-functional properties of food structures
- Encapsulation and delivery of bioactive compounds
- Enzyme isolation and characterization (aspartic proteases): extraction, purification, identification and activity quantification

## Methods and expertise
Protein and enzyme extraction, purification, identification and activity assays - 
Rheological, textural and microstructural characterization of food systems -
Techno-functional properties of proteins (gelation, hydration, particle size) - 
Microencapsulation, in vitro release and bioaccessibility studies - 
Experimental design and statistical data analysis
