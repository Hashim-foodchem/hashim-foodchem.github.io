Welcome to my platform for food science, technology, research, and innovation.
I am a food scientist and researcher with expertise in food protein functionality, food structure, ingredient development, and sustainable food technologies.
My work connects fundamental scientific understanding with practical applications in food product development, with particular interests in plant-based and hybrid foods, protein gelation, food texture, and functional ingredients.
Through this website, I share my research, scientific perspectives, educational resources, and insights into emerging developments in food science and technology.
## Research interests
- Food Proteins and Structure
- Food Processing and Ingredient Innovation
- Sustainable Food Innovation
- Data-Driven Food Science
